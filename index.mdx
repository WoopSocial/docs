WoopSocial is a social media management platform. You can use it to:
* Create and schedule posts on various social platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, LinkedIn Pages, Pinterest, TikTok, X (formerly Twitter) and YouTube through our UI
* Automate your workflows through our powerful API
* Give your AI agent the ability to manage your socials via our MCP

<Note>
  Using Claude or ChatGPT? Add WoopSocial from the [Claude directory](https://claude.ai/directory/woopsocial) or the [ChatGPT directory](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ae20d28f30c81919992b6b198b05d2b) and sign in. You don't need an API key. See the [Claude](/mcp/integrating-clients#claude-cowork) and [ChatGPT](/mcp/integrating-clients#chatgpt) setup steps.
</Note>
